Home Blood Collection, Blood Tests & Health Package FAQs

Find answers about test prices, preparation, reports, bookings and service locations.

How can I book home blood collection?

Select your city and choose Book Home Collection, or call/WhatsApp Patel Diagnostic at 8209746239 to request a sample collection appointment.

Can I check blood test prices online?

Use the test directory to search by test name, short code or sample type and review the listed price. Confirm availability and current pricing when booking.

What is included in a Lipid Profile Test?

A lipid profile commonly measures total cholesterol, HDL cholesterol, LDL cholesterol and triglycerides. The exact panel may vary; follow your clinician or laboratory's preparation instructions.

Which tests are related to diabetes screening?

Fasting blood glucose, random blood glucose and HbA1c are commonly used to assess blood sugar. A clinician can advise which test is appropriate for your situation.

Do I need fasting before a blood test?

Fasting depends on the specific test. Some glucose and lipid tests may require fasting, while many other tests do not. Confirm instructions before collection.

Can I book a full body health checkup package?

Review the health packages listed on this website for included tests and prices. Ask the booking team to confirm package contents, preparation and availability.

Do you provide home blood collection in Udaipur?

Patel Diagnostic is based around its Udaipur service. Select Udaipur and your locality or contact 8209746239 to confirm collection availability at your address.

Can I request blood collection in Jaipur, Jodhpur, Ahmedabad or Surat?

You can select a listed city or contact the team to ask about your location. Service coverage should be confirmed before booking; city selection does not guarantee availability in every locality.

How do I find CBC, thyroid, liver and kidney function test prices?

Search the Test Directory for CBC (Complete Blood Count), thyroid tests such as TSH, liver function tests (LFT) or kidney function tests (KFT) to view matching listings.

Can I send a doctor's prescription for test booking?

Use the prescription form to start a WhatsApp booking request. You may need to send the prescription file directly in the WhatsApp chat after it opens.

How are test results interpreted?

Reference ranges can vary by laboratory, method, age and other factors. Discuss results with a qualified healthcare professional rather than relying on a website alone.

Does choosing a city mean service is guaranteed there?

No. The selector helps identify your requested location. Please contact Patel Diagnostic to confirm actual collection availability and appointment timing for your address.
